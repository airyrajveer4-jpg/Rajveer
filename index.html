About Me

(I love all of it)
I don't pick one lane. I create in all of them.

I'm Rajveer, a BBA graduate from Raipur who turned curiosity into a full creative toolkit. I make AI videos and content, edit on PC and mobile, design visuals, build websites, and write articles and stories. Since March 2026 I've been part of the core team at AstroShrine, building its app, websites and content.

I love editing for other people and I shoot my own footage, because when I'm behind the camera the story is mine from the first frame. My real edge is the little things: the frame that's one beat late, the colour that's slightly off, the kerning nobody else noticed. I notice. Always.

Before all this I was running operations at a gas agency at 19 and editing at a local studio, so I know how business runs and how content hits. Off the clock it's football, Barça and my squad.
